# Is it worth it? (impact)

_Generated by `scripts/write_impact.py` from `results/bench_v3/daily.csv` and `config.yaml` → `impact`. Every cost is an ASSUMPTION (vendor quotes PENDING); the tariff is a stand-in (PENDING). Not a headline result._

## Central case (mid-point assumptions)

- Hardware for 9 building nodes: Rs 7.9 lakh; maintenance Rs 0.8 lakh per year.
- Bill saving vs today: Rs 16.3 lakh per year (mean of 30 RVCE days × 365).
- Extra battery wear from cycling the existing batteries: Rs 60.5 lakh per year (3,975 kWh more throughput per day × Rs 4.17/kWh).
- Net value per year: Rs -44.9 lakh.
- 10-year NPV at 8 %: Rs 96.4 lakh before battery wear, Rs -309.3 lakh after.
- Payback: 0.5 years before battery wear
; not reached after battery wear.
- Break-even battery wear: Rs 0.99 per kWh of throughput (the central assumption is Rs 4.17).

## What this means

- Every optimiser in the scorecard prices battery wear in its objective (`config.yaml` → `battery_wear`), so the batteries cycle only when a price spread beats the wear. An earlier run without that term cycled the batteries for small spreads and lost money after wear (`results/impact/wear_pilot_2.96.csv` documents the pilot that led to the change).
- Reliability (critical downtime) and resilience to a network failure are benefits not priced here.

## Scale path

- **RVCE pilot**: 9 buildings, one node each (this page).
- **Bengaluru campuses**: the same node per building; no campus-wide server (the benchmark's four other campuses).
  - RVCE, Bengaluru (case study): gap to the best plan 2.3 %, bill change +2.7 % (stand-in tariff), 2.2 s per decision
  - BDG2 university site 'Fox' (Arizona): gap to the best plan 1.5 %, bill change -6.0 % (stand-in tariff), 2.0 s per decision
  - BDG2 university site 'Bull' (Texas): gap to the best plan 2.7 %, bill change -5.3 % (stand-in tariff), 2.2 s per decision
  - BDG2 university site 'Cockatoo' (New York): gap to the best plan 4.7 %, bill change -5.6 % (stand-in tariff), 2.2 s per decision
  - CU-BEMS office building, Bangkok (7 floors): gap to the best plan 7.9 %, bill change +2.7 % (stand-in tariff), 2.5 s per decision
- **Feeders**: campuses and buildings on one distribution feeder agree the same way, with neighbours only (per-decision work and messages depend on a building's neighbours, not on the size of the network (consensus ADMM); not yet tested on a feeder model).
